PAPER 1 — HEREDITY (2022)

Recapitulating Population Structure via AIMs Panel

Khan et al. identified 4 major population clusters across India (North-East, North-West, South, and combined Terai + Central India) and developed a 92-SNP Ancestry Informative Marker panel to recapitulate whole-genome structure.

DOI: 10.1038/s41437-021-00477-y
PAPER 2 — PNAS (2021)

Inbreeding Depression & Purging of Deleterious Variation

Khan et al. analyzed 57 whole tiger genomes to demonstrate severe genomic inbreeding (F_ROH = 0.57) in isolated North-West tigers alongside purging of loss-of-function variants, but elevated burden of damaging missense mutations.

DOI: 10.1073/pnas.2023018118

    VERDANT Reproducibility Workflow Commands

    The exact sequence of computational commands for reproducing this pipeline:

    1. FastQC Quality Assessment
    conda create -n fastqc -c bioconda fastqc
    conda activate fastqc
    fastqc *.fq.gz
    2. Trim Galore Adapter & Quality Trimming
    conda create -n trim-galore -c bioconda trim-galore
    conda activate trim-galore
    trim_galore --paired --illumina BEN_CI16_sub_1.fq.gz BEN_CI16_sub_2.fq.gz
    3. BWA MEM Read Alignment
    conda create -n bwa -c bioconda bwa
    conda activate bwa
    bwa mem GCA_021130815.1_PanTigT.MC.v3_genomic.fna BEN_NW10_sub_1_val_1.fq.gz BEN_NW10_sub_2_val_2.fq.gz > BEN_NW_10_aligned_reads.sam
    4. Samtools SAM to Sorted BAM Conversion
    conda create -n samtools -c bioconda samtools
    conda activate samtools
    samtools view -S -b BEN_NW_10_aligned_reads.sam | samtools sort -o BEN_NW_10_sorted_reads.bam
    5. GATK4 MarkDuplicates
    conda create -n gatk4 -c bioconda gatk4
    conda activate gatk4
    gatk MarkDuplicates -I BEN_NW_10_sorted_reads.bam -O BEN_NW_10_deduplicated.bam -M BEN_NW_10_duplication_metrics.txt --REMOVE_DUPLICATES true
    6. Indexing & Alignment Statistics
    samtools index BEN_NW_10_deduplicated.bam
    parallel 'samtools stats {} > {.}_stats.txt' ::: *_deduplicated.bam
    7. Qualimap Coverage QC
    conda create -n qualimap -c bioconda qualimap
    conda activate qualimap
    qualimap bamqc -bam BEN_NW12_aligned_reads_sorted_deduplicated.bam -outdir qualimap_results -outformat HTML
    8. VCFtools Quality, Missingness & Depth Filtering
    conda create -n vcftools -c bioconda vcftools
    conda activate vcftools
    vcftools --vcf machali_...vcf --minQ 30 --minGQ 30 --hwe 0.05 --out machali_filtered --recode
    vcftools --vcf machali_filtered.recode.vcf --remove-indels --out machali_snps --recode
    vcftools --vcf machali_snps.recode.vcf --min-meanDP 12.8302 --max-meanDP 24.6604 --recode --out machali_mid95
