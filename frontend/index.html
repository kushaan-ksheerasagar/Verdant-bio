08. Reproducibility & Workflow Provenance

USER CLI PIPELINE

User Analysis Workflow Sequence:

FASTQ → FastQC → Trim Galore → BWA-MEM → SAMtools → GATK MarkDuplicates → Qualimap → VCFtools → PLINK / ADMIXTURE

Exact User Execution Commands

1. Tool: FastQC | Purpose: Quality Control
conda create -n fastqc -c bioconda fastqc
conda activate fastqc
fastqc *.fq.gz
2. Tool: Trim Galore | Purpose: Adapter & Quality Trimming
trim_galore --paired --illumina BEN_CI16_sub_1.fq.gz BEN_CI16_sub_2.fq.gz
3. Tool: BWA-MEM | Purpose: Reference Read Alignment
bwa mem GCA_021130815.1_PanTigT.MC.v3_genomic.fna BEN_NW10_sub_1_val_1.fq.gz BEN_NW10_sub_2_val_2.fq.gz > BEN_NW_10_aligned_reads.sam
4. Tool: SAMtools | Purpose: SAM to Sorted BAM Conversion
samtools view -S -b BEN_NW_10_aligned_reads.sam | samtools sort -o BEN_NW_10_sorted_reads.bam
5. Tool: GATK4 MarkDuplicates | Purpose: PCR Duplicate Removal
gatk MarkDuplicates -I BEN_NW_10_sorted_reads.bam -O BEN_NW_10_deduplicated.bam -M BEN_NW_10_duplication_metrics.txt --REMOVE_DUPLICATES true
6. Tool: Qualimap | Purpose: BAM Coverage QC
qualimap bamqc -bam BEN_NW12_aligned_reads_sorted_deduplicated.bam -outdir qualimap_results -outformat HTML
7. Tool: VCFtools | Purpose: Quality, Missingness & Depth Filters
vcftools --vcf machali.vcf --minQ 30 --minGQ 30 --hwe 0.05 --remove-indels --max-missing 0.6 --min-meanDP 12.8302 --max-meanDP 24.6604 --recode --out machali_filtered

09. Conservation Genomics & Management Guidelines

KHAN ET AL. (2022) HEREDITY

Evidence-based conservation management strategies translating population genomic structure into actionable wildlife management guidelines for wild tiger landscapes across India.

Defined Conservation Management Units (CMUs)

CMU 1: North-West Cluster

HIGH PRIORITY RESCUE

Protected Areas: Ranthambore Tiger Reserve & Sariska Tiger Reserve (Rajasthan)

Demonstrates extreme genetic differentiation (F_ST = 0.17–0.19) and high autozygosity (F_ROH = 0.570). Highly vulnerable to inbreeding depression and loss of adaptive potential.

Action: Assisted gene flow (translocation of unrelated Central Indian individuals) & corridor restoration to Ramgarh Vishdhari.

CMU 2: South India Cluster

CORRIDOR CONNECTIVITY

Protected Areas: Western Ghats Complex (Wayanad, Bandipur, Nagarhole, Mudumalai)

Moderately differentiated (F_ST = 0.10–0.13). Linearly connected landscape with moderate drift and accumulating long ROH segments in fragmented pockets.

Action: Maintain north-south ecological corridors across Nilgiri-Wayanad-Bandipur to prevent sub-cluster isolation.

CMU 3: North-East Cluster

DISTINCT LINEAGE

Protected Areas: Kaziranga Tiger Reserve & Floodplain Landscape (Assam)

Forms a distinct evolutionary lineage separated by historical isolation from peninsular populations (F_ST = 0.06–0.17). Unique allele frequencies across AIM markers.

Action: Designate as distinct management unit; preserve Brahmaputra riverine corridors and limit inter-regional translocations.

CMU 4: Central India & Terai Metapopulation

GENETIC RESERVOIR

Protected Areas: Kanha, Pench, Bandhavgarh, Tadoba-Andhari, Corbett

Largest connected metapopulation with highest observed heterozygosity (H_o = 0.00140) and lowest pairwise F_ST (0.03). Efficient purifying selection against deleterious mutations.

Action: Primary source landscape for genetic rescue translocations and baseline reference for non-invasive monitoring.

Conservation Action Framework Summary

Intervention TypeTarget LandscapeGenomic RationaleExpected Outcome
Assisted Gene Flow Ranthambore & Sariska (NW) Extreme inbreeding (F_ROH = 0.570), loss of heterozygosity Reduces autozygosity in F1 progeny by ~45%, mitigates inbreeding depression
Corridor Protection Nilgiri-Wayanad (Western Ghats) Linear geographic range causing bottlenecking Sustains gene flow between southern reserves without translocations
Non-Invasive Monitoring Pan-India Scat Samples 92-SNP Ancestry Informative Marker (AIM) Panel Rapid population assignment (99% accuracy) without invasive capture
