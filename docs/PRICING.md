# VERDANT Commercial & Pricing Structure

VERDANT operates on a hybrid conservation-academic tier structure:

1. **Academic & Non-Profit Wildlife Research**:
   - Free tier for open-source conservation initiatives and endangered species monitoring projects.
   - Standard community support, Zenodo integration, and open datasets.
2. **Conservation Agencies & Protected Area Authorities**:
   - Institutional licensing for national parks and forestry departments.
   - Dedicated private cloud / on-premise deployments, encrypted GPS masking, priority Nextflow compute batching.
3. **Commercial & Genomic Sequencing Partners**:
   - API quotas, high-throughput pipeline compute orchestration, SLA-backed multi-tenant deployments.
